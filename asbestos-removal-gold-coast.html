⚖ QLD Work Health & Safety Compliance

Under Queensland legislation, commercial property owners and body corporate managers on the Gold Coast are legally required to maintain an up-to-date Asbestos Register (s425) and Management Plan (s429) for all buildings constructed prior to 1990.
