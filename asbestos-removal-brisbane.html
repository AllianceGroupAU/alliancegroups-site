Direct Summary: Brisbane Asbestos Removal Compliance

  • ✓ Licensing Requirement: Any removal exceeding 10m² of bonded asbestos in Queensland must be performed by a licensed Class B removalist. Friable asbestos mandates a Class A license.
  • ✓ Builder Synergy: As a Licensed Removalists & Compliance Specialists, Alliance Group removes asbestos and immediately executes modern structural reinstatement (roofing, framing, lining).
  • ✓ Statutory Clearance: Every project concludes with an independent visual and atmospheric clearance certificate under WHS Regulation 2011 s473.

Complete Asbestos Removal Solutions in Brisbane

With over 60% of Brisbane's residential and commercial buildings constructed prior to 1990 containing asbestos-containing materials (ACM), safe and licensed removal is a vital safety and legal requirement. Alliance Group provides end-to-end hazard containment, licensed transport, and certified disposal.

⚖ Queensland Statutory Reference

Under the Work Health and Safety Regulation 2011 (Qld) and the Code of Practice: How to Safely Remove Asbestos 2021, duty holders must ensure asbestos removalists hold active WorkSafe QLD licenses, establish strict exclusion zones, and manifest all waste to EPA-approved facilities.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does asbestos removal cost in Brisbane?
Costs depend on accessibility, quantity, and material type. Small residential eaves or bathroom removals typically range from $850 to $2,500 + GST, while larger roof replacements or commercial fitouts are priced via custom on-site scope. Contact us for an upfront, fixed-price quote.
Can you replace the roof or walls after removing the asbestos?
Yes. Because Alliance Group is a Licensed Removalists & Compliance Specialists, we provide complete turn-key reconstruction—installing modern Colorbond roofing, timber/steel framing, insulation, and plasterboard linings.
