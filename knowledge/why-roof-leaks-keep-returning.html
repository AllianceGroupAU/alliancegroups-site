Why Roof Leaks Keep Returning

A forensic engineering breakdown of capillary water draw, differential air pressure, box gutter hydraulic overflow, and why topical silicone patches fail.

Book Roof Leak Investigation Building Ingress Diagnostics

The 3 Fundamental Causes of Recurring Roof Leaks

  • ✓ 1. Entry Point Does Not Equal Drip Point: Water enters at a ridge, flashing, or penetrations and runs down top-chords, foil sarking, or pipes for 15+ metres before dripping onto internal plaster.
  • ✓ 2. Capillary Action & Negative Pressure: Microscopic lap gaps draw water uphill during high-velocity wind storms where simple gravity drainage models do not apply.

Why Silicone Caulking is a Temporary Illusion

The most common mistake in roofing maintenance is hiring a handyman or trade to smear silicone sealant across leaking metal laps or tile valleys.

  • Thermal Movement Shear: Metal roofing sheets expand and contract significantly under Queensland's intense daily temperature swings (often 20°C–60°C on dark metal). Silicone has low shear tolerance and tears away from dusty sheet edges within months.
  • Trapped Moisture Acceleration: Topical silicone creates a dam that traps moisture inside the lap, accelerating electrolytic corrosion and hidden rust through-holes.

The 4 Hidden Structural Failure Modes

  1. Undersized Box Gutters: Built without emergency overflow weirs, causing water to back up under apron flashings during 1-in-20 year storm intensities.
  2. Un-turned Sheet Ribs: Failure to mechanically turn up sheet pans (stop-ends) at the ridge apex, allowing wind-driven rain to blow over the top edge.
  3. Degraded Neoprene Washers: Original tek screw washers perish after 10–15 years, allowing water to track down screw threads into timber trusses.
  4. Parapet Flashing Creep: Single-piece masonry cappings without continuous drip grooves, allowing water to track back along the underside into cavity walls.
