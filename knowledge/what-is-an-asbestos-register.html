What is an Asbestos Register?

An authoritative statutory breakdown of Queensland Asbestos Register obligations, mandatory inclusions, pre-2004 property triggers, and compliance duties for building managers.

Direct Statutory Definition

  • ✓ What It Is: An Asbestos Register is a live, statutory legal document that identifies and records all identified or assumed asbestos-containing materials (ACM) within a building constructed prior to 1 January 2004.
  • ✓ QLD Law: Under Section 425 of the Work Health and Safety Regulation 2011 (QLD), a Person Conducting a Business or Undertaking (PCBU) or Person in Management or Control of a Workplace (PMCW) must ensure a register is maintained and accessible to all workers and contractors.

When is an Asbestos Register Legally Required?

In Queensland, an Asbestos Register is mandatory for:

  • Commercial Buildings: Offices, retail shops, warehouses, factories, and hospitality venues built before 1 January 2004.
  • Industrial Complexes: Manufacturing facilities, storage yards, and distribution centers.
  • Strata Common Property: Residential body corporate schemes containing common property (stairwells, foyers, garages, roof cavities, plant rooms) built before 2004.
⚖ Statutory Penalty Warning

Failure to maintain an Asbestos Register or failing to provide it to trade contractors prior to maintenance or refurbishment carries substantial financial penalties under Part 8.3 of the Work Health and Safety Regulation 2011 (QLD).

What Must Be Included in an Asbestos Register?

According to the How to Manage and Control Asbestos in the Workplace Code of Practice 2021, a compliant register must state:

  1. Exact Location: Room, elevation, or building component (e.g., Level 2 switchboard backing, plant room eaves).
  2. Material Description & Type: E.g., chrysotile in vinyl floor tiles, amosite in electrical meter board.
  3. Friability Assessment: Whether the material is bonded (non-friable) or friable (readily pulverized).
  4. Condition & Disturbance Risk: Good condition, weathered, damaged, or unsealed.
  5. NATA Laboratory Results: Certified Polarized Light Microscopy (PLM) analysis reports verifying presence or presumption.

Register vs. Asbestos Management Plan: What is the Difference?

The Asbestos Register (s425) is the inventory (what is there and where it is located). The Asbestos Management Plan (s429) is the operational action plan (how risks are controlled, schedule of inspections, emergency incident protocols, and contractor handover workflows). Both are legally required together.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do residential houses require an Asbestos Register?

Purely domestic residential premises occupied solely as a home do not require a Section 425 register under WHS legislation. However, if tradespeople are engaged for major demolition or refurbishment, a pre-demolition survey is strongly recommended. Strata common property DOES require a register.
