Can Asbestos Roofing Be Repaired?

A definitive legal and engineering guide on Super Six asbestos roofing, high-pressure cleaning bans, repair limits, and the transition to Colorbond.

Asbestos Roof Removal Scope Request Replacement Quote

Direct Legal Summary

  • ✗ High-Pressure Cleaning is Illegal: Under Section 446 of the QLD WHS Regulation 2011, using pressure washers or mechanical grinding on asbestos roofs is a criminal breach subject to massive statutory fines.
  • ✓ Permanent Solution: Once an asbestos Super Six roof reaches its end of life (brittleness, moss growth, cracking), the only legally sound, long-term solution is licensed Class B removal and replacement with Colorbond steel.

Why Attempting to Patch Asbestos Roofs is Ineffective

Corrugated asbestos cement sheets (Super Six) installed across Queensland between 1940 and 1985 are now between 40 and 80 years old. Decades of subtropical rainfall, UV radiation, and hail have degraded the cement matrix, making sheets fragile and prone to sudden collapse under foot traffic.

  • Severe Fall Hazard: Weathered asbestos sheets are brittle. Stepping off the purlin line can cause instantaneous breakthrough and fatal falls.
  • Sealant Failure: Applying bituminous paint or silicone over mossy, oxidized asbestos sheets fails within 12–18 months because sealants cannot bond to friable substrate.
  • Expanding Fastener Holes: Original galvanized hook bolts corrode, expanding and cracking the surrounding asbestos sheet.
⚖ WorkSafe Queensland Legal Notice

It is unlawful to work on an asbestos roof without a Safe Work Method Statement (SWMS), fall arrest/crawl boards, and appropriate asbestos controls. Anyone caught water-blasting asbestos faces on-the-spot stop-work orders and severe environmental remediation cleanup orders.

The Compliant Replacement Workflow

  1. Class B Licensed Removal: Licensed removalists spray sheets with PVA bonding solution and carefully unbolt them without breakage.
  2. HEPA Cavity Vacuuming: Full vacuuming of ceiling timbers and insulation to remove decades of settled dust.
  3. Independent Clearance Inspection: Issuance of a Form Section 473 Clearance Certificate by an independent competent person.
  4. Purlin Upgrades & Colorbond Installation: Installation of heavy-duty Anticon foil insulation and BlueScope Colorbond sheeting.
