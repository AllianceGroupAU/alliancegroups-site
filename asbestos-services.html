Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if my Queensland property contains asbestos?

Any residential or commercial building constructed prior to 1990 in Australia is likely to contain asbestos materials in areas such as wet area wall linings, soffits, eaves, vinyl tile underlay, or corrugated roofing. The only definitive way to confirm is NATA-accredited laboratory testing.

How quickly do I get my test results?

Standard laboratory testing turnaround is 24–48 hours from receipt of the sample. Emergency priority same-day turnaround is also available upon request.

Are your removal teams licensed and insured?

Yes. Alliance Group and AUasbestos hold all required building practitioner licences, Class B/Class A removal certifications, and comprehensive public liability and workers compensation insurance.

When is an Asbestos Clearance Certificate required?

Under Queensland WHS Regulation 2011 s473, a clearance inspection and written certificate by a licensed independent assessor is legally mandatory following any licensed asbestos removal work before re-occupying the area.
