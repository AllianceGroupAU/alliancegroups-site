Asbestos services

Clear answers before you book.

Practical advice for homeowners, property managers and strata across Brisbane.

How can I tell if a material contains asbestos?

Appearance or age alone cannot confirm asbestos. Avoid cutting, drilling, sanding or disturbing suspect material. Have a suitable sample assessed through an accredited laboratory if identification is required.

Do you handle friable and non-friable asbestos?

Alliance offers Class A asbestos removal capability. The removal method, notifications, access controls and independent clearance requirements depend on the material and site conditions.

How much does asbestos removal cost?

Costs depend on the material, quantity, access, containment, disposal, clearance and any reinstatement. We explain whether an inspection is needed and confirm applicable fees before proceeding.

Can people stay in the property during removal?

Occupants must stay out of the asbestos removal work area. Whether other areas may remain occupied is determined by the risk assessment and the required separation and safety controls.

Will the job include a clearance certificate?

Clearance and handover requirements vary with the work. Where required, an independent competent person or licensed asbestos assessor provides the relevant documentation.

Can you complete building repairs afterwards?

Yes. Alliance can scope and carry out associated repairs and reinstatement after the required removal and clearance stages, subject to the agreed project scope.
