Privacy & your enquiry

We use the contact details, property information and supporting files you provide to assess and respond to your enquiry. Submit only information relevant to the work; avoid including identity documents or other unnecessary personal information.

Enquiries are processed through our Google Firebase service and notification emails are addressed to info@alliancegroups.com.au. Supporting files are stored with the enquiry. Website analytics record page visits and actions such as enquiry submissions and contact-link clicks; form answers and uploaded files are not sent to analytics.

For questions about your information, email info@alliancegroups.com.au or call 0403 126 276.
