Property manager question

Who repairs roof leaks for rental and strata properties in Brisbane?

Alliance Group investigates roof leaks for rental, strata, residential and commercial properties, identifies the likely entry point and carries out the practical repair work.

Property manager question

What happens when a roof leak damages internal linings?

Alliance Group can manage roof repairs, damaged ceiling or wall linings, water-affected materials and reinstatement as part of the same property response.

Property manager question

Can Alliance Group attend storm-damaged roofs?

Alliance Group attends storm-damaged roofs, provides temporary protection where required and completes the repair or replacement work once the condition is assessed.
