Asbestos roofs.

Corrugated asbestos cement roofing was common on Queensland homes, garages, sheds and carports until the 1980s. After decades in the sun it weathers, cracks and becomes brittle — and that is when leaks start.

Do not water-blast it

High-pressure cleaning, cutting with power tools, sanding and drilling all disturb asbestos cement. Neither should anyone walk on old sheeting — it can give way. Leave it until it has been assessed.

Because Alliance holds asbestos removal capability and does the roofing, an asbestos roof replacement is one job: notifications, controlled removal, licensed disposal, clearance where required, then the new roof, gutters and flashings.

Roofing questions

Roofing, plainly answered.

Still unsure? Call 0403 126 276.

Can you replace an asbestos roof? +

Yes. Asbestos cement roof sheeting is removed under the required licence and controls, waste is disposed of at a licensed facility, and we install the new roof. One contractor handles removal and replacement, so there is no gap between trades.

Should an old asbestos roof be repaired or replaced? +

It depends on the condition of the sheeting and what the owner wants to achieve. Weathered or broken asbestos sheeting should not be water-blasted, cut or walked on carelessly. We look at the condition and give options before any work is done.

Do you find the cause of a roof leak? +

Yes. We inspect the roof, flashings, gutters and likely entry points, then repair the cause rather than only patching the stain on the ceiling.

Can you repair the ceiling damage from a leak? +

Yes. Water-damaged ceilings, linings and insulation can be removed and replaced as part of the same job once the roof is watertight.

Do you attend storm-damaged roofs? +

Yes. We provide temporary protection such as tarping where required, then complete the repair or replacement once the condition is assessed. See emergency response.
