Asbestos removal across the Redlands.

From Capalaba and Alexandra Hills to the bayside suburbs of Wellington Point, Ormiston, Cleveland and Victoria Point, the Redlands has a large stock of homes, beach houses and outbuildings from the era of asbestos cement sheeting. Coastal weather is also hard on old roofs and eaves.

We remove asbestos sheeting, roofs, eaves and damaged material under licence, and complete the repair or new roof afterwards.

In Queensland, a licence is not needed to remove 10 m² or less of non-friable (bonded) asbestos — but the work still has to follow the Work Health and Safety Regulation, and the waste still has to be disposed of properly. More than 10 m² of bonded asbestos needs a Class B licence; any friable asbestos needs Class A. How the rules work.

Asbestos waste in Redlands.

The national asbestos disposal facility finder lists two Redland City facilities:

  • Birkdale Waste Transfer Station — residential and commercial asbestos, with a limit of less than 10 m² or 250 kg.
  • Redland Bay Recycling and Waste Centre — residential asbestos only, with the same less than 10 m² or 250 kg limit.

Anything bigger than that, or any broken or friable material, is a job for a licensed removalist — who handles the wrapping, transport, disposal and waste paperwork.
