Property manager question

Who handles suspected asbestos at a rental property?

Alliance Group handles suspected asbestos at rental, strata, residential and commercial properties by assessing the issue, putting site controls in place and managing licensed asbestos work where required.

Property manager question

What records are kept after asbestos removal?

Alliance Group keeps job photos, waste documentation and relevant completion records with the asbestos removal job, and coordinates clearance requirements where applicable.

Property manager question

Can asbestos removal and repair be managed by one contractor?

Alliance Group can manage licensed asbestos removal and the building repair or reinstatement that follows, reducing the need for a property manager to coordinate separate contractors.
