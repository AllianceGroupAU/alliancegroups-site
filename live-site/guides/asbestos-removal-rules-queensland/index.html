Friable and non-friable.

Queensland’s rules turn on what kind of asbestos it is.

  • Non-friable (bonded) asbestos is bound into another material — most commonly asbestos cement sheeting (“fibro”), roofing and pipes. In good condition it does not crumble by hand.
  • Friable asbestos is loose, or can be crumbled, pulverised or reduced to powder by hand pressure when dry. It releases fibres much more easily. Bonded material that is badly damaged — by fire, for example — can become friable.

The 10 m² rule.

WorkSafe Queensland: a licence is not required to remove ten square metres or less of non-friable asbestos. That is roughly a small bathroom wall or a couple of sheets of eave lining.

Small does not mean anything goes

Even under 10 m², the work must still follow Part 8.7 of the Work Health and Safety Regulation 2011, and the waste must be disposed of properly — QBCC warns that hefty fines apply. The 10 m² limit never applies to friable asbestos.

Notifications and clearance.

Before licensed asbestos removal work starts, a notification must be submitted to Workplace Health and Safety Queensland. For urgent removal after a flood or severe storm there is a time-limited exemption — see asbestos after a storm or flood.

When the removal is finished, the area is inspected before it is reoccupied. For friable asbestos, a licensed asbestos assessor carries out air monitoring and the clearance inspection, and issues the clearance certificate. For bonded work, the clearance inspection is carried out by an independent competent person.

Checking a licence.

WorkSafe Queensland publishes registers of Class A and Class B licence holders and recommends checking the register to confirm a business holds the licence it claims. It is worth doing for any removalist — including us.

Disposal.

Asbestos waste has to go to a facility that accepts it, wrapped and handled correctly. Council rules vary a lot across South East Queensland — Brisbane City Council and Ipswich City Council resource recovery centres do not accept asbestos, while Logan and the Redlands have facilities with load limits. See the local details on our area pages.

Questions

Quick answers.

Still unsure? Call 0403 126 276.

Can I remove asbestos myself in Queensland? +

You can remove 10 m² or less of non-friable (bonded) asbestos without a licence, provided the work follows Part 8.7 of the WHS Regulation 2011 and the waste is disposed of properly. Anything over 10 m², and any friable asbestos, needs a licensed removalist.

Which licence do I need for an asbestos roof? +

Asbestos cement roofing is usually non-friable, and most roofs are well over 10 m², so a Class B (or Class A) licence is needed. If the sheeting is badly damaged it may be friable, which needs Class A.

Who issues the clearance certificate? +

For friable asbestos removal, a licensed asbestos assessor carries out air monitoring and the clearance inspection and issues the certificate. For bonded work, the clearance inspection is done by an independent competent person.
