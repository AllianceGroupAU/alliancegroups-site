Before we get there.

Suspected asbestos disturbed or damaged?

Keep people and pets away. Do not sweep, vacuum, water-blast or walk through broken sheeting. If it is safe, close windows and doors to the area.

Power, gas or structural risk?

Stay out of the building if there is any doubt, keep clear of fallen lines and call 000 or your energy provider first.

Damaged asbestos.

Storms do not care what a house is made of. Hail and wind break asbestos cement roof sheeting and fibro, and flood debris can carry broken material through a yard. Broken bonded sheeting is more likely to release fibres than intact sheeting, so it needs to be controlled before the clean-up starts.

Queensland allows licensed removalists to carry out urgent removal of damaged asbestos that poses an imminent risk to the community straight after a flood or severe storm, under a time-limited class exemption, with notification to the regulator afterwards. Removal later on — for example months later as part of an insurance claim — goes through normal advance notice. Our storm and flood guide covers the details.

Emergency questions

When it cannot wait.

Still unsure? Call 0403 126 276.

Do you attend after hours? +

Yes — emergency make-safe calls are taken 24/7. Call 0403 126 276 and tell us whether the property is safe to enter, whether anyone is hurt, and what has been damaged. For immediate danger, call 000 first.

What does a make-safe include? +

A make-safe controls the immediate risk: isolating damaged areas, temporary weatherproofing such as tarping, securing loose or broken material, and stopping further water entry. It is followed by a scope for the permanent repair.

Can asbestos be removed straight away after a storm? +

Where damaged asbestos poses an imminent risk to the community if it is not immediately removed, Queensland has a class exemption that lets licensed removalists carry out urgent removal without the usual advance notice, with notification afterwards. It only applies to genuinely urgent work after the event. More on asbestos after storms.

Will you document the damage for insurance? +

Yes. We photograph the condition found and the make-safe work, and keep job notes with the record so the owner, property manager or insurer can see what was done and why.

Do you do the repair as well? +

Yes. Once the immediate risk is controlled we can scope and complete the roofing, building repair and reinstatement work — the same contractor from make-safe to finished repair.
