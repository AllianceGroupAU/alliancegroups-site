Property manager questions

For the people who get the call.

Still unsure? Call 0403 126 276.

A tenant says there is asbestos. What do I do first? +

Ask the tenant to keep away from the material and not to disturb it, get photos and the location, and send them to us. We will tell you whether it needs testing, a make-safe or removal, and what the tenant needs to know in the meantime.

Can you deal with the tenant directly? +

Yes, if you want us to. We can arrange access with the tenant and keep you updated on what was found and what happens next.

Do you send photos and records? +

Yes. Condition photos, job notes, waste documentation and completion records are kept with the job, so you have something to put on the property file.

Can you handle the repair after removal? +

Yes. Alliance can carry the job through removal, clearance coordination, repair and reinstatement, so you are not chasing separate contractors.

Do you work with strata and body corporate managers? +

Yes. We work on common property and lots, and can provide the records committees need. For a wider records review, see Compliance Shield.
