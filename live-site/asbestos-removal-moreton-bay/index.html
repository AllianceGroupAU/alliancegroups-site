Asbestos removal across Moreton Bay.

Moreton Bay runs from the established suburbs on Brisbane’s northern edge — Albany Creek, Strathpine, Lawnton and Petrie — out to the Redcliffe Peninsula and up through Burpengary, Morayfield and Caboolture. Plenty of homes, sheds and garages across the region were built before 1990, when asbestos cement sheeting was a standard building product.

We remove fibro walls and ceilings, asbestos cement roofs, eaves, splashbacks, vinyl underlay and damaged material after storms, and we can carry the job through to the new linings, roof or repair.

In Queensland, a licence is not needed to remove 10 m² or less of non-friable (bonded) asbestos — but the work still has to follow the Work Health and Safety Regulation, and the waste still has to be disposed of properly. More than 10 m² of bonded asbestos needs a Class B licence; any friable asbestos needs Class A. How the rules work.

Asbestos waste in Moreton Bay.

The national asbestos disposal facility finder lists two Moreton Bay facilities that accept both residential and commercial asbestos loads: the Dakabin Waste Management Facility and the Caboolture Waste Management Facility. Both list 24 hours’ notice as a requirement, so do not turn up unannounced.

Asbestos must be wrapped and handled correctly before it is transported. If the job is more than a small amount of bonded sheeting, or the material is broken or friable, it is safer and simpler to have it removed and disposed of by a licensed removalist — the waste documentation then comes with the job.
