Quick answers

Mould & Remediation
in plain English.

Each answer states the property issue, the work Alliance Group carries out and the practical next step.

Property manager question

Who handles mould caused by a roof leak or water ingress?

Alliance Group handles mould and water-damaged building materials by identifying the affected area, removing or remediating what is required and completing the repair work afterwards.

Property manager question

Does mould remediation include repairing damaged walls and ceilings?

Alliance Group can carry mould remediation through to replacement linings, repairs and reinstatement where the property needs building work after the affected materials are dealt with.

Property manager question

What should a property manager send for a mould or water-damage issue?

Property managers can send the address, photos and a short description of the mould or water damage so Alliance Group can work out the practical next step.
