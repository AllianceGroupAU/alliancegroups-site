What we do.

  • Moisture-source investigation — roof leaks, plumbing, water ingress, condensation and poor ventilation.
  • Mould remediation — containment, removal of affected materials and decontamination cleaning.
  • Water damage and flood response — wet linings and floors opened up, affected materials removed, drying set up.
  • Drying and moisture control — making sure the structure is dry before anything is closed back in.
  • Reinstatement and repair — new linings, finishes and joinery so the area is usable again.

Older buildings.

Mould and water damage in a pre-1990 building often sits behind fibro wall or ceiling sheeting. Cutting it out without checking can turn a mould job into an asbestos exposure. We check first, and if asbestos is involved we can remove it under licence as part of the same job.

Mould questions

Mould and water damage, plainly answered.

Still unsure? Call 0403 126 276.

Will cleaning the mould fix the problem? +

Not on its own. Mould keeps coming back while the moisture source is still there. We find and fix the source — a leak, water ingress, poor ventilation or flood damage — then deal with the affected materials.

Do you remove mouldy plasterboard and carpet? +

Yes. Materials that cannot be properly cleaned are removed under containment, the area is decontaminated and dried, then new linings and finishes go back in.

Can mould remediation and repairs be one job? +

Yes. Alliance carries mould remediation through to replacement linings, repairs and reinstatement, so the room is ready to use again.

What if the damaged walls might contain asbestos? +

In older homes, water-damaged linings may be fibro. We check before cutting into anything, and because we are licensed for asbestos removal, the job does not stall waiting for another contractor.

What should a property manager send? +

The address, a few photos of the affected area and a short description. That is enough for us to work out the practical next step.
