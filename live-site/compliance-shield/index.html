How it works.

  1. Overview. A short conversation about the site, what records exist and where they live.
  2. Baseline Compliance Review. We review the site’s photos, registers, history and contractor controls, and show you what is current, what is missing and what should be tightened up.
  3. Ongoing oversight, if needed. Keeping the record current as work happens on site — only if it is useful to you.

What it builds.

  • A current photo record of the site condition, not a folder of old images.
  • Registers that match the site — checked against what is actually there.
  • Site history — what work was done, where and when.
  • Contractor controls — who worked on site and under what controls.
Why it matters

When something goes wrong, or someone asks for proof, the question is always the same: what is on site, and who touched it? Compliance Shield is about being able to answer that without digging through five inboxes.

What is a Baseline Compliance Review? +

It is the first step: a review of the site’s current photos, registers, history and contractor controls so you can see what is current, what is missing and what should be tightened up.

Who is Compliance Shield for? +

Churches, childcare, aged care, retirement living, community facilities, property managers and strata — sites where people need to be able to show what is on site and who has worked there.

Do we have to sign up for ongoing oversight? +

No. Start with the overview and the Baseline Compliance Review. Ongoing oversight is only there if you need it.
