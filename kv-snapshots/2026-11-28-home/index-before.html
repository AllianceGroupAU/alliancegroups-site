Built before 1990? Read this first.

If a Queensland home was built before 1990, it probably has some asbestos in it. Get it checked before anyone drills, cuts or sands.

Before 1990: likely to contain asbestos

If a home was built before 1990, it is likely to contain some asbestos materials. Leave suspect material alone until it has been checked, and plan any renovation around it.

1990 – 2003: less likely, still possible

Asbestos was banned in building products in 1990, but some homes built in the 1990s and early 2000s still used asbestos products until the total ban in 2003. Check before you renovate.

After 2003: unlikely in the original build

The total ban came into effect in 2003. Older sections, extensions or reused materials can still contain asbestos, so check anything that pre-dates the build.

07 — Asbestos services

Clear answers before you book.

Practical advice for homeowners, property managers and strata across Brisbane. Still unsure? Call 0403 126 276.

How can I tell if a material contains asbestos? +

Appearance or age alone cannot confirm asbestos. Avoid cutting, drilling, sanding or disturbing suspect material. Have a suitable sample assessed through an accredited laboratory if identification is required.

Do you handle friable and non-friable asbestos? +

Yes. We handle bonded asbestos jobs from first inspection to completion paperwork, and arrange friable asbestos removal through licensed partners. The method, notifications and the final inspection depend on the material and the site.

How much does asbestos removal cost? +

Costs depend on the material, quantity, access, containment, disposal and the final inspection. We explain whether an inspection is needed and confirm applicable fees before proceeding.

Can people stay in the property during removal? +

Occupants must stay out of the asbestos removal work area. Whether other areas may remain occupied is determined by the risk assessment and the required separation and safety controls.

Do you organise the completion paperwork? +

Yes. We organise the independent final inspection. After licensed removal, an independent licensed asbestos assessor (or, for some bonded asbestos work, an independent competent person) inspects the area and issues the completion paperwork before it is reoccupied. See asbestos completion paperwork.
