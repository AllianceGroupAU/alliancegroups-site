Commercial Roofing Highlights

  • ✓ Hydraulic Box Gutter Sizing: We engineer oversized box gutters with overflow sumps compliant with AS/NZS 3500.3 to prevent torrential storm inundation.
  • ✓ Tenancy Protection: Fast-tracked installation protocols that protect plant equipment, inventory, and electrical assets during roof renewal.

Specialist Commercial Roofing Services

  • Industrial Metal Re-Roofing: Replacement of deteriorated corrugated steel with long-span 0.48mm BMT Colorbond or Zincalume profiles.
  • Box Gutter & Rainwater Engineering: Custom stainless steel and Colorbond box gutters engineered for maximum storm throughput.
  • HVAC & Penetration Flashing: Custom welded penetrations, Dektite seals, and step flashings around roof-mounted plant equipment.
  • Parapet & Capping Rectification: Custom folded parapet cappings, expansion joint covers, and continuous apron flashings.
