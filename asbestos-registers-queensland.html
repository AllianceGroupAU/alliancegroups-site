Statutory Asbestos Registers Queensland (s425)

Legally certified asbestos registers and hazardous material audits for commercial properties, body corporates, industrial facilities, and schools under QLD WHS Regulation 2011.

Direct Summary: Queensland Asbestos Register Mandate

  • ✓ Legal Obligation (s425): All Queensland commercial buildings and residential common properties constructed before 31 December 1989 MUST maintain a compliant Asbestos Register.
  • ✓ Contractor Access: Tradespeople and contractors must review and sign the on-site register before commencing any repair, maintenance, or fitout works.
  • ✓ Penalties: Failure to maintain or provide an Asbestos Register carries statutory penalties under the Work Health and Safety Act 2011 (Qld) of up to $30,000 for individuals and $150,000 for corporations.

What Does an Alliance Group Asbestos Register Contain?

Our licensed hazmat assessors conduct an exhaustive visual and physical audit of the property, taking selective samples for NATA-accredited polarized light microscopy (PLM) analysis. The resulting register includes:

  • Exact Location Mapping: Annotated building plans identifying the exact rooms, elevations, and structural zones containing ACM.
  • Material Type & Classification: Identification of Chrysotile (white), Amosite (brown), or Crocidolite (blue) asbestos in bonded or friable state.
  • Condition & Disturbance Risk: Numerical risk matrix evaluating material degradation, accessibility, and likelihood of fiber release.
  • NATA Laboratory Certificates: Official attached analytical reports proving material composition.
  • Recommended Control Actions: Clear prioritization for encapsulation, labeling, regular monitoring, or licensed removal.
⚖ Statutory Legal Notice (s425 & s429)

If asbestos is identified or assumed present in a commercial building or common property, the PCBU must also prepare a written Asbestos Management Plan (s429) outlining the ongoing safety measures and 5-year inspection cycles.

Asbestos Register vs. Management Plan Comparison

Frequently Asked Questions

Do residential rental properties require an asbestos register in QLD?
Individual private residential homes solely occupied as dwellings are exempt under Section 425. However, if the residential property contains shared common property (e.g. strata apartment stairwells, clubhouses, common carports), the body corporate is legally classified as a PCBU and must hold an asbestos register.
How quickly can Alliance Group inspect and deliver an Asbestos Register?
Standard turnaround is 3–5 business days following on-site sampling. For urgent commercial property transactions or emergency council compliance notices, express 24–48 hour priority delivery is available.
