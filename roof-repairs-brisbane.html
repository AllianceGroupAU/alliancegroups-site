Why Brisbane Roofs Leak (And Why Quick Patches Fail)

Brisbane's sub-tropical climate exposes roofs to extreme thermal cycling (up to 70°C roof surface temperatures) followed by intense high-volume storm deluge. Most recurring leaks stem not from simple broken tiles, but from inadequate lap expansion, undersized box gutters, rusted valley irons, or failing penetrations.

Common Roofing Systems & Rectification Protocols

Roof Material Primary Failure Mode Diagnostic Method Rectification Protocol
Commercial Metal / Colorbond Fastener washer degradation, sheet lap capillary draw, parapet flashing failure. Thermal imaging, flood testing, fastener torque assessment. Class 4 screw replacement, custom gauge Colorbond flashings, box gutter resizing.
Concrete / Terracotta Tile Cracked mortar bedding, displaced ridge caps, broken tiles, blocked valley irons. Visual structural survey, thermal leak tracing in ceiling space. Flexible acrylic pointing, tile replacement, Colorbond valley iron renewal.
Asbestos Super Six Roofing Brittle panels, hairline stress cracks, storm water ingress, fiber release. Hazmat visual survey & NATA testing validation. Licensed Class B removal, purlin upgrade, and modern insulated Colorbond installation.
