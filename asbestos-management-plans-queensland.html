Asbestos Management Plans Queensland (s429)

Comprehensive statutory Asbestos Management Plans (AMP) engineered for commercial property managers, body corporates, and facility directors under QLD WHS Regulation 2011 Section 429.

AMP Statutory Requirements Summary

  • ✓ Mandatory Trigger (s429): If an Asbestos Register reveals or assumes the presence of asbestos, a written Asbestos Management Plan MUST be established and maintained.
  • ✓ 5-Year Review Cycle: Under QLD law, the AMP must be formally reviewed at least once every 5 years, or immediately whenever ACM is disturbed or removed.
  • ✓ Contractor Protocols: Establishes clear permit-to-work guidelines for electricians, plumbers, and maintenance trades before touching building elements.

Core Elements of an Alliance Group Asbestos Management Plan

An Asbestos Management Plan is not merely a static report—it is an active operational safety document. Alliance Group develops structured asbestos management plans aligned to Queensland WHS requirements, with gaps and actions clearly recorded, detailing:

  • Decisions & Reasons for Risk Control: Why specific materials are managed in-situ versus scheduled for removal or encapsulation.
  • Incident & Emergency Procedures: Step-by-step protocols in the event of accidental ACM disturbance, storm damage, or ceiling collapse.
  • Contractor Induction & Sign-In Workflow: Clear rules requiring all visiting trades to review the register and adhere to non-destructive drilling/cutting practices.
  • Roles & Responsibilities: Explicit assignment of duty holders (PCBU, property manager, maintenance coordinator).
  • Timetable for Action & Re-Inspection: Condition monitoring schedule to evaluate material weathering over time.
