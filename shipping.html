Service Delivery & Shipping Policy

Last updated: August 2026 | Physical product dispatch and on-site service timelines

1. Scope of Service Delivery

This policy details the delivery timelines, dispatch processes, and turnaround times for professional on-site trade services, compliance audits, and NATA laboratory testing provided by Alliance Group Pty Ltd and AUasbestos across Brisbane and South East Queensland.

2. On-Site Inspection & Hazard Audits

  • Booking Confirmation: Once an on-site inspection or quote is requested via our portal or phone, our operations desk contacts you within 24 hours to confirm scheduled technician arrival time.
  • Service Coverage: Brisbane Metro, Moreton Bay, Logan, Redlands, Ipswich, Gold Coast, and Sunshine Coast.
  • Emergency Response: 24/7 priority emergency make-safe response is available across Greater Brisbane for acute structural or hazardous material emergencies.

3. Laboratory Testing & Certificate Turnaround

  • Standard Testing: Once physical samples are collected by our licensed technician and submitted to our partner NATA-accredited laboratory, formal PDF Certificates of Analysis are dispatched via email within 24 to 48 hours.
  • Priority / Same-Day Turnaround: Same-day laboratory turnaround is available for urgent commercial property transactions or emergency clearance inspections.
  • Clearance Certificates: Issued and delivered digitally on the same day as the physical clearance inspection once independent air/visual standards are met.

4. Compliance Shield™ Reviews

The Compliance Shield™ $395 + GST document audit is delivered digitally within 48 hours of receiving all required statutory property files, building plans, and previous registers.
